mcp-yoto

Use your Yoto library from Claude, ChatGPT or any other AI app that supports custom connectors — make a card, add a chapter, upload audio, browse the pixel-art icons, check what is on your players.

Works with Yoto. This is an independent, open-source project by a parent; it is not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Yoto.

How signing in works

When you connect, this server sends you straight to Yoto’s own login page — you type your Yoto password on Yoto’s site, never here. Yoto then hands back a key that is stored encrypted, locked to your AI app, so the person running this server cannot read it. You can disconnect in your AI app, or remove access at Yoto, whenever you like.

It asks only for permission to see and manage your own cards, icons and family library, and to see your players. It never asks for permission to control a player, so it can never start, stop or change what your child is listening to.

Before you connect

  • You need a Yoto account and at least one Make Your Own card to get the most from it.
  • Uploading audio from a chat app works by web address, not by file — a chat app has no access to your computer’s files.
  • Anything you ask it to change is a real change to your real Yoto account.
